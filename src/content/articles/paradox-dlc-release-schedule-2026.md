Paradox Interactive runs on a DLC treadmill, and 2026 is shaping up to be a busy year. With EU5 in its first full year, CK3 continuing its expansion cycle, and EU4 still getting support, there's a lot to track. Here's what we know about the 2026 DLC schedule.

## Europa Universalis V

EU5 launched in November 2025, and Paradox has already confirmed a full DLC roadmap for 2026. Here's what's been announced:

### Fate of the Phoenix — Released May 6, 2026

The first EU5 immersion pack, **Fate of the Phoenix** focuses on the Byzantine Empire. It adds new mechanics, events, and flavor for one of the most iconic powers in the game's timeline. If you're playing as the Byzantines — or planning to restore the Roman Empire — this is the pack for you.

### Across the Pillars — Q3 2026

The first EU5 Chronicle Pack, **Across the Pillars** covers the Strait of Gibraltar region, with content for Morocco and Castile. Chronicle Packs are Paradox's smaller content packs, typically adding new events, decisions, and regional flavor.

### The Auld Alliance — Q4 2026

The second EU5 Chronicle Pack, **The Auld Alliance** focuses on France, Scotland, and England — the tangled web of alliances and rivalries that shaped Western Europe. Expect new events and decisions tied to the Hundred Years' War era and its aftermath.

## Crusader Kings III

CK3 has been in a strong DLC cycle since 2020, and 2026 should continue that trend. The most recent major expansion was **Roads to Power** (2024), which added the landless adventurer playstyle.

**What to expect:** Paradox typically releases one major expansion and one or two flavor packs per year for CK3. The community is speculating about a few possibilities — a deeper republic system, more content for the Byzantine Empire, or a new region focus. Nothing has been officially confirmed for 2026 yet.

## Europa Universalis IV

EU4 is the interesting one. With EU5 out, you might expect EU4's DLC cycle to wind down — but Paradox has kept supporting it. The game still has an active playerbase, and Paradox has shown no signs of ending support.

**What to expect:** EU4's DLC releases have slowed compared to its peak, but the game still gets updates and the occasional content pack. Whether 2026 brings a new EU4 DLC is uncertain, but the game remains supported.

## How to Track Releases

The best way to stay on top of Paradox DLC announcements:

- **Paradox's dev diaries** — posted on the Paradox forums and Steam, these are the primary source for DLC announcements
- **The Paradox YouTube channel** — release trailers and announcement videos are posted here
- **Steam** — DLC pages go up on Steam before release, so you can see what's coming
- **The Paradox forums** — the community is always discussing upcoming content

## The Bottom Line

2026 is a big year for Paradox grand strategy. EU5 is getting its first DLC wave — Fate of the Phoenix is out now, with Across the Pillars and The Auld Alliance coming later in the year. CK3 is continuing its expansion cycle, and EU4 remains supported. If you're a fan of any of these games, there's something to look forward to.
