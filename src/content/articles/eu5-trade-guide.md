Trade in Europa Universalis V is one of the most important systems in the game, and it's also one of the most different from EU4. If you're coming from EU4, you'll need to unlearn some habits — the trade system has been significantly reworked. Here's what you need to know to get started.

## How Trade Works in EU5

The core concept is similar to EU4: goods are produced in provinces, flow through trade routes, and generate income. But the implementation is different. The key thing to understand is that trade is about **connecting production to demand**. Provinces produce goods, and those goods flow to places where they're needed. Your job as a ruler is to position yourself to capture value from that flow.

## Merchants

Merchants are your agents in the trade system. They're assigned to trade routes and trade centers, and they help you capture more value from the goods flowing through your realm.

[VERIFY: How merchants are obtained in EU5 — whether they're generated by trade buildings, ideas, or something else. In EU4, merchants came from trade ideas, trade companies, and certain nations. EU5 may have a different system.]

**The basics:**
- Assign merchants to the most valuable trade routes you have access to
- Merchants in your home region are more effective than those far away
- You can move merchants between routes, but there's a cooldown

## Building a Trade Economy

Here's a practical approach to building a strong trade economy as a beginner:

**1. Focus on your home region first.** Before you expand trade networks abroad, make sure your home region is well-connected. Build trade buildings in your capital and key provinces.

**2. Control production.** Trade is only as valuable as the goods being produced. Invest in production buildings in provinces with valuable goods — cloth, wine, iron, and other high-value resources.

**3. Expand into trade routes.** As you conquer territory, look for provinces that sit on important trade routes. Controlling the route is often more valuable than controlling the production.

**4. Use your merchants wisely.** Don't spread your merchants thin. Focus them on the 2-3 most valuable routes and move them only when you have a clear reason.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

- **Ignoring trade entirely.** Trade income can be a huge portion of your economy. Even a small investment in trade buildings pays off.
- **Spreading merchants too thin.** One merchant on a minor route is worth less than two merchants on your best route.
- **Not building production.** Trade without production is like a shop with nothing to sell. Build both.
- **Expanding into poor trade territory.** Not all conquests are equal. Prioritize provinces that add trade value.

## The Bottom Line

Trade in EU5 is a system that rewards attention. You don't need to master every detail to benefit — just understand that production feeds trade, trade feeds income, and merchants are your tools for capturing that income. Build your home economy first, expand into good trade territory, and keep your merchants focused on the best routes.

As you play more, you'll develop a feel for which routes are worth fighting for. But for now, these basics will keep your economy healthy while you learn the rest of the game.
